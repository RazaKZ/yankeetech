FLAGSHIP CASE STUDY SPOTLIGHT VERIFIED RESULTS

Direct-to-Consumer Apparel Brand ($3.4M Annual GMV)

The Challenge: The client was losing thousands of dollars every week due to high mobile checkout abandonment (74%) and slow WooCommerce product filtering.

The Solution: Yankee Tech stripped 32 redundant plugins, replaced them with a lightweight custom theme, engineered a 1-step checkout, and integrated 2-way GoHighLevel abandoned cart SMS triggers.

Measurable Business Impact
  • Cart abandonment plummeted from 74% to 46% within 14 days.
  • Mobile PageSpeed score surged from 38/100 to 98/100.
  • Recovered an additional $42,000 in monthly abandoned cart revenue.
