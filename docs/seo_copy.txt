Keyword data caveat. I can't pull exact search volume or CPC (that needs Semrush,
Ahrefs or Google Keyword Planner). The keywords below come from how top-ranking
pages and competitors actually describe these services right now, plus buyer intent.

Planner is free inside a Google Ads account.
What the search results showed. For GoHighLevel setup / expert, the results were
mostly Fiverr gigs and small agency pages. Broad terms like 'GoHighLevel expert' are
crowded with cheap gig-style listings, so don't rely on them alone. Aim at specific,
higher-value combinations: GHL + WordPress integration, white-label delivery, AI
voice agents, n8n / Make automation. That is also where your positioning is strongest.
Only publish real proof. The current draft has numbers, case studies and testimonials
that are placeholders. Replace them with real projects before launch. Fake proof
hurts trust with buyers and can get ads disapproved.
Domain. Publish on yankeeetech.com (root domain), not a subdomain.

pages don't compete with each other.

4. On-page and technical checklist (for your SEO friend)
One H1 per page, matching the primary keyword naturally. Title tags under 60
characters, descriptions under 155.
Internal links: Home links to every service page; each service page links to 2 related
services and to Contact; blog posts link back to the matching service page.
Schema markup: Organization on Home/About, Service on service pages, FAQPage
where FAQs appear.
Real Privacy Policy and Terms pages (the current links point to #home). Google Ads
requires these.
Submit sitemap to Google Search Console, set up GA4, and track form submits and
call bookings as conversions.
Fix placeholder links (blog posts, Calendly) and update the 'Accepting Q1 2026'
banner.
Compress images, use descriptive alt text, and aim for strong Core Web Vitals.
Add a Portfolio page with 3-5 real case studies in this format: problem, what we built,
result [real number].
