Laravel or Node.js for Your Business Application?

"Consider your workflows, existing stack and maintenance needs before choosing a backend."

Start with your application requirements

Choosing a backend starts with the product. Map the workflows, integrations and operational requirements, then consider the skills of the team that will maintain it.

Where Laravel fits

Laravel is a PHP framework used for applications such as portals, dashboards and business systems. When a project relies on structured data and recurring business workflows, it is one of the options we consider.

Where Node.js fits

Node.js lets teams use JavaScript on the server. It can fit projects with an existing JavaScript codebase, custom API services or requirements that benefit from shared language expertise across the team.

Review the complete system

Database design, permissions, background jobs, monitoring and deployment all affect the finished product. Decide how these pieces will be built and supported before committing to an implementation.

Bring the decision back to your team

Document your expected workloads, existing infrastructure and support responsibilities. We review those constraints with you and explain the tradeoffs behind the proposed architecture.
