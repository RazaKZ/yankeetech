2. Discovery, Scoping & Fixed Pricing

We operate on clear, milestone-based scopes with no surprise bills:

  • Initial Consultation: Free 30-minute technical evaluation to discuss requirements, architecture, and preliminary feasibility.
  • Scoping Phase: Dedicated discovery workshops and wireframe reviews. When a paid scoping deposit ($100–$250) is taken, 100% of the amount is credited toward your development milestone invoice upon project start.
  • Fixed Milestone Proposals: Each build includes a written specification, weekly sprint milestones, and an agreed fixed cost before code is written.

3. Milestone Payments & Invoicing

Standard fixed-price projects are structured into predictable milestone phases:

  • 30% Upfront Deposit: Secures engineering capacity, environment setup, and architecture initialization.
  • 30% Midpoint Review: Billed upon demonstration of working core workflows and API integrations.
  • 40% Final Launch Milestone: Due upon completion of QA testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), and before final production deployment.
  • Retainers & Dedicated Pods: Dedicated developer or agency white-label retainers are billed monthly in advance.

  • We never implement vendor lock-in or proprietary licensing gates.
